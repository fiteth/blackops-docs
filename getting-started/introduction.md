# Introduction

Blackops Capital has two subscriber tools:

* **Alpha Feed** shows signals and feed controls.
* **Wallets** lets you filter and review tracked wallet data.

Subscriber navigation includes **Alpha Feed**, **Wallets**, and **Docs**. Open **Account** from the user menu.

## Get started

1. [Create your account](account.md)
2. Open **Alpha Feed** and use its filters to find signals.
3. Use [Wallet Analytics](../features/wallets.md) to research wallets.
