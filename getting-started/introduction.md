# Introduction

Blackops Capital is a crypto analytics platform that helps you find alpha before the crowd.

## What You Get

- **Real-time alerts** when opportunities arise
- **Wallet analytics** to research top performers
- **Backtesting tools** to validate strategies
- **Paper trading** to practice without risk
- **API access** to build your own tools

## Supported Chains

- Solana
- Ethereum
- Base
- BSC
- Polymarket
