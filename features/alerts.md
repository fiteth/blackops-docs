# Alpha Alerts

Real-time notifications when opportunities arise on-chain.

## How It Works

Our system monitors on-chain activity 24/7 and alerts you when specific conditions are met. Each alert is scored and categorized.

## Alert Dashboard

The dashboard shows:

- Live alerts as they happen
- Historical alerts with performance data
- Filtering by type, time, and gains
- Sorting by various metrics

## Performance Tracking

Every alert tracks:

- Entry price at time of alert
- Current price (updated live)
- All-time high since alert
- Percentage gains

## Telegram Alerts

Get alerts sent directly to Telegram for immediate notification.
